## English (en) — 默认
名称：SuiYuan Search

简短描述：A clean new tab with clock, capsule search, wallpaper library, Bing daily and a dock.

详细描述：
SuiYuan Search turns the browser's new tab into a clean, customizable start page with a large clock, capsule search, wallpaper background and a dock of favorite sites.
- Clock: selectable font weight, synchronized colon blinking, 12/24-hour formats, optional seconds and date.
- Search: Bing, Google and Baidu, custom engines, Ctrl+1~9 switching, aggregated suggestions and optional local history; typed URLs open directly.
- Wallpapers: solid colors, gradients, built-in images, local images and videos, focal point selection, opacity, blur and dark overlay.
- Bing daily: preview, copyright, today/shuffle, download and 4K toggle; downloads are saved to the local library.
- Dock: up to 10 shortcuts; "..." opens a panel with 5-7 columns and 5 rows. Add, edit, reorder, move to top or delete shortcuts, import browser bookmarks (read-only) and import/export JSON.
- Themes and languages: follow system/light/dark; English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, French, Russian, Spanish, Arabic (RTL).
- Privacy: no accounts, tracking or ads; settings and media stay on the device. Network requests are limited to search suggestions and Bing wallpapers.
